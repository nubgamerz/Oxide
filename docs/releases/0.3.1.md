## Renamed effects

The built-in effects have plainer names. Nothing about how they sound has changed.

| before | now |
|---|---|
| Single-band Compressor | Compressor |
| Dynamics Processing | Dynamics |
| Hard Limiter | Limiter |
| DeEsser | De-esser |
| DeHummer | Hum Remover |
| DeClipper | Clip Repair |
| Automatic Click Remover | Click Remover |
| Click/Pop Eliminator | Click & Pop Repair |
| Adaptive Noise Reduction | Auto Noise Reduction |
| Studio Reverb | Room Reverb |
| Full Reverb | Space Reverb |
| Analog Delay | Vintage Delay |
| Parametric Equalizer | Parametric EQ |
| Graphic Equalizer (10, 20 and 30 Bands) | Graphic EQ (10, 20 and 30 bands) |

In the plug-in browser the built-in effects are now grouped as Dynamics, EQ, Restoration,
Reverb, Delay and Modulation.

The Diagnostics pane's finders are now Click Repair and Clip Repair.

Nothing to do after updating: old projects, templates, backups, rack presets and the saved
effects rack open and sound exactly as before. The next time you save, they use the new names.

For AI control: the effects' ids changed with their names (the old ids and names are still
accepted), the note-preview command is now `preview_note`, and the Diagnostics' kinds are
`click-repair` and `clip-repair` (`declicker` and `declipper` are still accepted).
