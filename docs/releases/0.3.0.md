This is the first public release of Oxide, a lightweight DAW for the bedroom and home musician:
record, edit, mix and master in one app. Open the manual from **Help > Manual**, or press **F1**
anywhere to land on the page about the part of Oxide you are pointing at.

## New in 0.3.0

**The manual is built in.** It is a searchable guide that works offline, in your browser.

- Pictures with the part they talk about outlined and numbered.
- F1 opens the page for where you are: a pane, a dialog, a settings page or a built-in effect.
- A guide for each stage: getting started, recording, the Multitrack, the piano roll, the mixer,
  automation, the Waveform view, exporting, rendering, projects and settings.
- A page for every built-in effect, with a table of all its controls.
- A troubleshooting chapter for choppy audio, no sound, plug-in problems and slow opens.
- Keyboard and mouse reference, plus a glossary.
- An MCP section for AI assistants such as Claude: every action Oxide offers, with an example,
  and an assistant can search the manual itself and open the page that answers your question.

## Also new since the last builds

These were never published on their own (0.2.4 to 0.2.8), so they are listed here.

**Heavy plug-ins and sound**
- Amp-sim plug-ins on drivers with tiny buffers: **Settings > Audio > Processing block** gathers
  small device buffers into a bigger block, so they stay in time without dropouts. The extra
  latency is shown there.
- Waveforms zoom in down to single samples, drawn as a line with dots. Both views use the same drawing.
- Claude keeps Oxide listed while it is closed, and its tools work as soon as you start Oxide.

**Editing clips**
- **Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V** copy, cut and paste clips (and audio in the Waveform view). A
  pasted clip plays exactly like the original.
- Grab the clip edge you mean: the selected clip's edge first, and edges under another clip can be grabbed.
- A press on an edge or fade handle changes nothing until you move, so a click never nudges a clip.
- A click on a clip places the playhead, as a click on an empty lane does.
- Double-clicks open clips again (a click had been getting in the way of them).

**Moving around**
- **Alt+scroll** changes the track heights around the pointer, so the track you are on stays put.
- A fast playhead drag now pulls the view along instead of stalling at the edge.
- A click in the piano roll's note grid places the playhead, as its ruler does.

## Good to know

- Windows only: 64-bit, developed and tested on Windows 11. Plug-ins are VST3.
- The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may ask once. Choose **More info**,
  then **Run anyway**.
- It installs for your account only (no administrator). Running a newer installer later updates
  Oxide in place and keeps your settings.
