## What's fixed

- **Copy and paste notes in the piano roll.** Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X in the piano roll now copy or
  cut the selected notes, and Ctrl+V pastes them at the playhead, the first note exactly where
  the playhead is and the others in the same places around it. Before, these keys copied the
  whole MIDI clip and pasted a copy of the clip instead. The Edit menu's Cut, Copy and Paste do
  the same after a click in the piano roll.

For AI control: `copy_notes`, `cut_notes` and `paste_notes` do the same for agents.
